Problem

UK SMEs are structurally underserved in employment law.

Micro-businesses regularly face dismissal uncertainty, contractor misclassification, holiday pay disputes, sickness absence issues, and ACAS or tribunal threats. Existing options are expensive, fragmented, generic, or unreliable when owners need a fast answer, risk orientation, and practical next steps.

What breaks today

Solicitors can be slow and intimidating, HR consultants vary widely, and generic guidance rarely fits the facts.

What employers need

Rapid reassurance, structured risk classification, and clear actions before a staff issue escalates.

What Axniks is building

A mobile-first decision-support layer that sits between HR software, legal services, and compliance automation.

Product

Ratio is WhatsApp-native employment guidance.

Employers explain a workplace problem in plain English. Ratio asks structured follow-up questions, classifies risk, generates practical guidance, and routes higher-risk cases to human support or partners.

User flow

  • Describe the issue in plain language.
  • Answer targeted follow-up questions.
  • Receive structured risk guidance.
  • Pay to unlock deeper analysis and workflows.
  • Escalate high-risk matters when needed.

Business model

  • Free risk triage.
  • £3–£15 premium guidance.
  • Document and workflow upsells.
  • Later: subscriptions, accountant and insurer partnerships, solicitor referrals.

Market

Massive fragmented SME base.

The UK has roughly 5.5M private sector businesses, most of them micro-businesses. Millions employ casual, temporary, or informal workers in sectors such as construction, hospitality, retail, logistics, care, and beauty.

These are exactly the businesses most exposed to employment uncertainty and least likely to have ready access to legal support.

Advantage

Messaging-native, SME-specific, workflow-oriented.

Ratio combines structured triage with human-supervised automation. Over time it accumulates proprietary intake data, escalation patterns, risk classifications, conversion signals, and workflow intelligence.

Operational moat Training dataset Automation advantage

Go-to-market

Start narrow, scale through intent.

Initial distribution focuses on high-intent search queries such as “can I fire employee”, “employee threatening tribunal”, and “self employed or employee”, then expands through SEO, accountant partnerships, payroll integrations, insurer channels, and short-form educational content.

Validation goals

Prove SMEs will pay for rapid guidance, identify repetitive query patterns, and improve automation rates.

Key metrics

Paid conversion, repeatability of queries, average handling time, automation rate, and CAC vs LTV.

Long-term vision

Begin with employment-risk triage and grow into AI operational-risk infrastructure for tax, compliance, insurance, and regulation.
