Problem

Small business owners face employment risk at the worst possible moments.

Employment issues often arrive suddenly: a discrimination letter after an advert, a disciplinary dispute, an ACAS claim, or a tribunal threat. At that point, many owners either guess, delay, or pay for support they cannot sustain. Too often, legal help costs the same as the claim value itself, so owners are forced into avoidable settlements. Generic templates miss case facts, and enterprise HR tooling is not built for how small teams actually work.1

Solution

Immediate triage now, then legal support when the risk is real.

Ratio delivers instant WhatsApp triage from plain-language descriptions, then supports escalation with deeper analysis and solicitor pathways where required. This gives owners reduced employment risk and access to legal services at a level that would normally be out of reach. Ratio exists specifically for this gap.

Opportunity

A large, underserved market at the right moment.

Micro businesses in the UK employ millions of casual and informal workers.2 Employment disputes are rising, legal costs are not falling and LLMs now make structured, and factual guidance economically viable for the first time. Ratio is built for exactly this window as a focused employment-risk service for this specific, underserved client group.

Ratio's models are trained and evaluated on Dawn, the Cambridge AI supercomputer, through a UKRI AI Research Resource grant — giving Ratio access to frontier-scale compute from day one.
