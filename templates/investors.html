Problem

Micro-businesses face employment risk with no good options.

Dismissals, contractor disputes, tribunal threats, and ACAS claims are routine for small UK businesses. Solicitors are expensive, HR platforms are built for larger firms, and generic guidance rarely fits the facts. Owners make costly decisions without proper support.

Solution

Instant, structured guidance over WhatsApp.

Free triage, then fee-based pay-as-you-go or subscription for deeper analysis, generated documents, and clear next steps. Higher-risk cases route to vetted human support.

Opportunity

A large, underserved market at the right moment.

Micro-businesses in the UK employ millions of casual and informal workers. Employment disputes are rising, legal costs are not falling and LLMs now make factual, structured guidance economically viable for the first time. Ratio is built for this window as a focused employment-risk service for an underserved segment.

Ratio's models are trained and evaluated on Dawn, the Cambridge AI supercomputer, through a UKRI AI Research Resource grant.
